Data model

The tables behind the screens. Outlined in blue: the parts no listing site has, which make this tool different.

buildings

Address, site link, platform the scraper reads, building shape (closed-loop or not).

floor_plans

Plan name, beds, baths, sq ft range, image and its fingerprint so changes are caught.

units

Unit number, floor, top floor, facing direction, faces the courtyard, what the window looks at.

availability_windows

Every time a plan or unit opens and closes, with rents seen. Answers "when was this last available?"

labels

Kitchen shape, walk-in shower, ADA, bedroom door. Records who said it (model, you, crowd) and whether it's been reviewed.

watches → alerts_sent

"Tell me when Milan opens on floor 4 or above under $1,500." Never alerts twice for the same opening.

fees + household

Pet, parking and admin fees, multiplied by the number of pets and cars in your household, for the true monthly cost.

location_scores

Coffee within a 10-minute walk, distance to trails, commute minutes to places you choose.

scrape_runs

Each check of each site. A failed or changed run tells you the scraper broke.
